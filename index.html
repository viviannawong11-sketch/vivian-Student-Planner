      About Student Planner

      Student Planner helps students organise their study tasks and assignments in one place. Add a task with a subject, tick it off when it is done, change it back if you need to, and delete it when you no longer need it. Your tasks stay saved in your browser even after you refresh the page.

      Features

      • Add tasks with a title and subject or course
      • Pending and Completed sections
      • Mark complete and undo
      • Delete tasks
      • Saved automatically with Local Storage
      • Responsive on laptops, tablets and phones
